afterwrd

A small social network of your own

Post, share photos and talk with friends who have their own afterwrd site, and, if you like, bring your Facebook years along. It runs in your own free Cloudflare account, so no one else holds your data and there's nothing to pay. A few minutes, on a phone or a computer.

Want the details first? How it works, or how afterwrd is different from Facebook. Questions, or something wrong? Help.

  1. Make a free Cloudflare account

    Cloudflare is where your site will run. It only needs an email address: you don't need to add a card, as afterwrd fits in the free plan. Confirm your email when Cloudflare's message comes, then come back here.

    Sign up to Cloudflare

    Already have an account just for afterwrd? Sign in. If you use Cloudflare for other things, make a separate one for afterwrd.

  2. Install

    Signed in to Cloudflare in this browser? Press Install.

    Cloudflare will ask you to Allow afterwrd to set up your site, which takes a few minutes. afterwrd's access runs out by itself, and it keeps nothing.

    Install

    On the way you give your name. Then you make a passkey to sign in with: your face, fingerprint or phone PIN, no password. Your site gives you a recovery code to keep somewhere safe. Last, it offers to keep itself up to date and to let friends' sites check it's genuine. You can say yes, or turn them on later.

  3. Bring your Facebook years (optional, any time)

    Ask Facebook for a copy of your things, in its Download your information: choose your profile, All available information, format JSON and media quality High. Facebook emails you when it's ready (minutes, or up to a day). Then choose Import in afterwrd's menu (behind your picture) and pick the zip file(s). What you publish appears under Memories, and friends you approve can see its posts and photos. Publish your Facebook friends list too, and it helps you see who to invite.

Keeping it safe

  • Look after your Cloudflare account as you would your email: your site lives there. Turn on two-step sign-in (My Profile → Authentication).
  • Keep your recovery code written down or in your password manager. Your site doesn't keep it, and if you lose every device, it's how you show it's still you.
  • Open device links only on your own phone or computer. A device link signs a new device in as you. Each one works once, within an hour.
  • Say yes only to people you know. Nothing is shared with anyone until you approve them as a friend.
  • Let it update itself, so fixes reach your site. If you said no during setup, turn it on from the Update page.
  • Home tells you when a new device is added. If it wasn't you, remove it in Your devices.

Locations are taken out of the photos you post. More in Is it secure?
