Connect your afterwrd site to Cloudflare?

This lets your site update itself (or your groups) for one hour. Check the address above is your own site: Cloudflare's next page won't show it. If it isn't yours, close this page.

What exactly does it allow?

For that hour, the site can make and change Workers (the small programs websites run on) and databases in your Cloudflare account. Nothing is kept afterwards.

Let your afterwrd site keep itself up to date?

New afterwrd releases will install by themselves, a few days after they come out. This stays on until you turn it off, on your site's Update page.

On Cloudflare's next page, leave Background Access ticked and press Allow. Only continue if the address above is your own site.

What exactly does it allow?

Lasting access to change your Workers (Cloudflare calls it Workers Editor), and nothing else: it can't make or read databases, read your account's settings or make tokens. You can also remove it in Cloudflare: My Profile → Access Management → Connected Applications.

Let your site look after itself?

  • Keep itself up to date. New afterwrd releases install by themselves, so you get fixes without doing anything.
  • Let friends' sites check it. Now and then, a friend's afterwrd site checks yours is the genuine afterwrd, and tells you if anything's wrong. It can only look, never change anything.

Both stay on until you turn them off, on your site's Update page. You can also say no on Cloudflare's page, and turn them on later.

On each of the next two Cloudflare pages, leave Background Access ticked and press Allow. Only continue if the address above is your own site.

What exactly do they allow?

Unlike the one-hour access that installed afterwrd, these last until you turn them off.

Let your friends' afterwrd sites check yours?

Now and then, a friend's afterwrd site checks yours is the genuine afterwrd, and tells you if anything's wrong. It can only look, never change anything. This stays on until you turn it off, on your site's Update page.

On Cloudflare's next page, leave Background Access ticked and press Allow. Only continue if the address above is your own site.

What exactly does it allow?

Lasting access to read the code of every Worker in your Cloudflare account (not only afterwrd's), and nothing else: it can't change anything, or read your databases or your account's settings. Your site gives a friend's site that asks a one-hour, read-only pass from it. You can also remove it in Cloudflare: My Profile → Access Management → Connected Applications.

Let this friend's afterwrd site check yours?

It looks at your site's code, and at the record Cloudflare keeps of changes to your account, to check your site is the genuine afterwrd. It can only read, never change anything, and the access ends after an hour. Only continue if this is the site of a friend who offered to check yours.

Get back into your afterwrd site?

Cloudflare will ask to let afterwrd change your Workers for an hour. Your site uses that once, to prove you're its owner, without changing anything, and keeps nothing. Then you can add the device you're on. Only continue if this is your own site, and you asked for this: nobody else, friends included, ever needs it.
